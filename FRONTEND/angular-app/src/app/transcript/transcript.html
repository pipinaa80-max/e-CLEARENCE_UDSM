Charges and mode of payment

The first transcript request costs TSh 15,000. Each additional transcript request costs TSh 5,000.

For non-Tanzanian nationals, the transcript charge is US$20 for the first copy and US$10 for each additional copy.

Payment is made through the University payment control number. Keep the payment receipt for submission during the next step.

Required documentation

Attach the required identification and supporting documents requested by the University, including a valid passport-size photograph and proof of payment where applicable.

Make sure all information is accurate. Incomplete or incorrect requests may be returned for correction.
