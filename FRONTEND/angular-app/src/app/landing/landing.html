How Clearance Works

1

Student Request

Students log in with their registration credentials to initiate the digital clearance process.

2

Department Approval

Academic and administrative units review records and approve pending items online.

3

Multi-Unit Signoff

Library, Finance, ICT, and Academic Directorate clear obligations seamlessly.

4

Final Clearance Certificate

Download your official exit clearance certificate upon completing all approval stages.
