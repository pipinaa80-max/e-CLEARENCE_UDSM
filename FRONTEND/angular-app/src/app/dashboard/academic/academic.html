UDSM Academic Clearance Roles & Responsibilities

How academic units collaborate during student graduation clearance.

Course Lecturers

Register and resolve student equipment, book, apparatus, or coursework liabilities via the Lecturer Debt Register. Open flags automatically lock Stage 3 Department approval.

Department Officers (HoD)

Review Stage 3 departmental clearance after Convocation and Parallel Offices approve, verifying zero open Lecturer debt flags before forwarding to the College Principal.

Research Supervisors

Perform the Stage 6 final academic and undergraduate certificate check for Postgraduate candidates (Master, PhD, PGD) after Finance approval.
